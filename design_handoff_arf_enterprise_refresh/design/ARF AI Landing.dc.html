How the commercial model works

ARF is not sold per seat. Governance value scales with the volume and consequence of the decisions being governed, so the model has two parts.

01

Fixed deployment fee

Integration into your infrastructure, policy authoring, and the initial control-plane configuration. One-time, scoped in writing.

02

Outcome-based or retainer

Then either a share of measured risk reduction, or a flat monthly retainer. You choose which at the end of the pilot.

03

Pilot costs nothing

Time-limited and free for qualified organisations. No commitment, no auto-conversion — the commercial conversation happens after.

1Enterprise overhaul — three whole-page directions

Type scale

Instrument Sans replaces the Tailwind default — a refined grotesk that holds tight tracking at display sizes. One scale: 56 / 34 / 21 / 17 / 14.5 / 11 mono. Headlines 600–700 at −0.024em; body 17px at 1.62 line-height. Newsreader italic appears once, in the pull quote — the only serif on the page, so it reads as a deliberate editorial moment rather than decoration. JetBrains Mono carries eyebrows, labels and code, which is what makes an infrastructure product feel technical without a dark dashboard.

Spacing rhythm

The old page used py-20 everywhere, so nothing read as more important than anything else. New rhythm alternates deliberately: hero 128px → trust bar 72px (tight, scannable) → problem 112px → why 96px → industries 80px → architecture 128px → capabilities 120px → governance 128px (the proof section gets the most air) → quote band 104px → pricing 112px → explore 104px. Cards no longer nest inside cards; sections sit directly on the canvas and are separated by whitespace and hairlines, not borders.

Colour

Light is primary. Warm off-white canvas #FAF9F7 with warm-gray ink #191816 / #56534D, muted tier #6B6862 (all AA at 12px+) — never pure black on pure white, which reads cheap at scale. The recognisable blue-purple survives as a single accent gradient (#3358E8 → #7A4BE0) used in exactly three places per page: the hero, the testimonial band, and interactive accents. Semantic colour (red/green/yellow) is retired from decoration and kept only for state. Dark mode inverts canvas and surface against the same accent.

Enterprise positioning

Nav drops 8 links to 4 + two buttons, with Sign In outlined and pilot-access filled — the shape of a product you log into, not a repo you browse. Community links, the version badge and two of three sandbox disclaimers move to the footer or die. Trust moves above the fold as a three-column bar. A quote band and a dominant Enterprise pricing column supply the credibility signals buyers scan for in three seconds.
